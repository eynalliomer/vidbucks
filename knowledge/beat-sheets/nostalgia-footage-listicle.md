# Nostalgia footage listicle format - beat sheets (slots, no material copied)

## 10-minute version
| time | beat | picture | on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:22 | HOOK: three concrete images, each with a {year} or {price} from a cited source; one kid's-eye detail | 1-s cuts over period footage/photos | big number pops ({price} in the accent colour) |
| 0:22-0:27 | PROMISE: "So let's take a trip back and remember {the promise in the title}" | one slow wide shot | none |
| 0:27-0:31 | BUMPER: channel name card | own logo on a textured dark ground | channel name |
| 0:31-9:20 | 12-16 ITEMS x 30-45 s. Each: (a) what it was, (b) who had it / how it felt, (c) one funny twist, (d) a "today" callback | 3-5 s shots: footage, object photo, inset-on-grid every ~3rd item | chapter pill lower-left for the whole item; one number pop per item at most |
| ~5:00 | mid CTA | — | "Share your memories in the comments" pill, 4 s |
| 9:20-9:45 | wrap: "Most of these are ordinary now / gone, but back then ..." | slow montage of earlier shots | none |
| 9:45-10:05 | OUTRO: question to the audience + subscribe + "more on the channel" | logo card, subscribe button animation | — |

## 60-second proof version (for testing the build)
| time | beat |
|---|---|
| 0-12 s | hook: 3 images in 1-2 s cuts, one number pop |
| 12-15 s | promise line |
| 15-18 s | bumper |
| 18-52 s | 2 items x ~17 s, each with its chapter pill, one inset-on-grid shot |
| 52-60 s | outro question + subscribe |

## Script rules for this format
- 155-180 wpm, "we / us / your friend's dad", short sentences, one joke per item, no hype words.
- Every year, price or share comes from a cited source written in the script notes; otherwise drop the number.
- Item order: strongest first after the hook, second-strongest last before the outro.
