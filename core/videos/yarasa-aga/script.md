# Yarasa Ağa - Shorts v1 (Tudor format, long takes)
Title idea: "Osmanlı'da Gece Kahramanı Olmak, 1592 (Yarasa Ağa Vlog)"
Original character inspired by the bat-vigilante archetype; no DC names, logo, emblem or costume copy.
Format: 9:16, 8 clips x 8 s (~64 s), Omni 1.1 Flash 360p, 48 credits. [FRAME] = start frame is the last frame of the previous clip (continuous take).

Comedy engine (from Tudor analysis): myth/self-image hook, deadpan pauses, off-screen filmer gag (apprentice Rıza), everybody in the neighbourhood knows his "secret identity", modern comparison (light signal), outro question + CTA joke.

Characters: YARASA (reference image), Rıza (young apprentice, films, never seen), neighbourhood night watchman with a drum, two old women at a window, a cat.

## Look block (start of every YARASA prompt)
Vertical 9:16 smartphone vlog that looks like a real phone recording made at night in 1592 Ottoman Istanbul, but no phone is ever visible: natural uncorrected colour, moonlight and warm oil-lamp light, slight noise in the shadows, not cinematic. YARASA exactly as his reference: Turkish man about 40, round friendly face, short black beard and mustache, home-sewn black leather half-mask over eyes and nose, black felt cap with two short stitched pointed ear tips, long black cape with a scalloped bat-wing hem, black quilted kaftan, brown leather belt with pouches and a small brass lantern. No emblem or logo anywhere. Continuous handheld take, small reframes, autofocus breathing. Deadpan humour: he tries to sound dark and mysterious and fails; Turkish speech at a natural pace, lips synced, talking or reacting until the end. Ambient sound only. No music, no subtitles, no names, no writing or letters anywhere, no modern objects.

## Take A - rooftop (continuous)
1 [INGREDIENT] Night, a wooden rooftop in Galata with the moon and the stone Galata tower behind him. Arm's-length selfie, low angle, wind in the cape. In a deep forced whisper: "Selam olsun, İstanbul. Ben Yarasa Ağa." Pause. "Gecenin gölgesi." Beat, normal voice: "Gündüzleri yorgancıyım." He spreads the cape dramatically and the wind blows it over his face; he fights it off. Sound: wind, a distant dog.
2 [FRAME 1] Same shot, no cut: he points down: "Şehir beni çağırıyor." He flips the phone to show the dark street below: a single cat looks up and meows. He flips back, flat: "Bir kedi." Beat. "Olsun. Görev görevdir." Sound: cat meow, wind.
3 [FRAME 2] Same shot, no cut: he hands the phone to someone off-screen: "Rıza, çek." The view shakes as it changes hands, now third person: he steps to the roof edge, spreads the cape like wings and jumps; the camera rushes to the edge and looks down: he crashes into a pile of fish baskets in the street. From below, a groan: "İyiyim." Sound: wind, crash, baskets.

## Take B - street (hard cut in)
4 [INGREDIENT] Narrow cobbled street at night, oil lamps. Third-person phone held by an apprentice, shaky. YARASA limps, a small fish stuck on his shoulder. An old night watchman with a drum and a lantern blocks his way. YARASA, deep voice: "Dur! Kimsin sen?" Watchman, calm: "Bekçiyim. Sen kimsin?" YARASA: "Ben... gecenin..." Watchman: "Yorgancı Hasan değil misin?" Long pause. YARASA: "Değilim." Sound: footsteps, a cricket.
5 [FRAME 4] Same shot, no cut: the watchman shrugs and walks away. YARASA turns to the camera and whispers: "Gizli kimliğim." Beat. "Mahallede herkes biliyor." Beat. "Ama kimse söylemiyor." Above him a window shutter opens. Sound: shutter creak.

## Cutaway (hard cut, no YARASA reference)
6 [no ingredient] Vertical phone video at night looking up at a wooden Ottoman house window in Istanbul, 1592: two old women in white headscarves lean out holding an oil lamp and giggle. One says in Turkish: "Hasan yine kılık değiştirmiş." The other: "Geçen hafta tavuğu kurtardı." They giggle and close the shutter. Natural lamp light, slight shake. Sound: giggles, shutter. No music, no subtitles, no writing anywhere.

## Take C - signal and outro (hard cut in)
7 [INGREDIENT] Selfie again, a small rooftop. He holds up his brass lantern with a bat-shaped paper cut-out in front of the flame; a faint bat-shaped shadow appears on the misty sky. Proudly: "Sizin şehirlerinizde kahramanı ışıkla çağırıyorlarmış." Beat. "Bizde de var." The flame flickers and goes out; darkness. Pause. "Mum bitti." Sound: wind, flame hiss.
8 [FRAME 7] Same shot, no cut: dawn light rises, a call to prayer far away; he pulls the mask up onto his forehead and yawns: "Yorumlara yazın: sizin mahallenin kahramanı kim?" Beat. "Abone olun. Yoksa gece gelirim." Pause. "Gündüz olmaz. Gündüz yorgan dikiyorum." Sound: distant call to prayer, birds.

## Notes
- Galata Tower existed in 1592 (stone tower). Bekçi (night watchman) is a real Ottoman office. All characters fictional.
