# Portremi Yaptırıyorum, 1480 (Fatih Sultan Mehmet Vlog) - 1 min
Format from our shot review of the Tudor "Armada" video: talking selfie ~75 %, third-person ~12 %, B-roll ~13 %; cold open in extreme close-up; long takes continued by extending the previous clip.
16:9, Omni 1.1 Flash, 360p, 8 x 8 s = 48 credits.

History: Gentile Bellini was sent by Venice and worked in Istanbul 1479-1481; his portrait of Mehmed II is dated 25 Nov 1480 and hangs in the National Gallery, London (arch frame, crowns). Mehmed was 48, with a famous aquiline nose; he suffered from gout. Grapes, page, "three months" are comic licence.

Character (FATIH 1480): man of 48, strong hooked aquiline nose, full dark-brown beard with grey threads, heavy-lidded tired eyes, slightly full face; large white turban over a small red cap; red silk kaftan with a wide dark fur collar; rings.

1 [INGREDIENT] COLD OPEN - Palace room, soft window light. Extreme close-up selfie, the phone so close his eyes and the big nose fill the frame. He whispers without moving his face: "Selam olsun, kullarım." Beat. "Kıpırdamamam lazım." He pulls the phone back a little: "Venedik'ten ressam getirttim. Yüzümü çiziyor."
2 [EXTEND 1] Same take: he turns the phone to show a Venetian painter in a black cap at a wooden easel, brush in hand, who snaps in Italian: "Sultano, non muovere!" He turns the phone back, deadpan: "Kıpırdama diyor." Beat. "Bana."
3 [NO REF] B-roll close-ups: brush mixing oil paint on a wooden palette, bright pigments, a canvas with the outline of a man in profile under an arch (no letters), window light, dust in the air. VO: "Siz buna selfie diyorsunuz." Beat. "Bizde üç ay sürüyor."
4 [INGREDIENT] Selfie, close, he sits very stiff, a bead of sweat; his eyes cross down at his nose: "Burnum kaşınıyor." Beat. "Bir haftadır." He does not move.
5 [EXTEND 4] Same take: a servant enters with a silver plate of grapes; the painter shouts "No!" and waves him away; the servant freezes and backs out. The sultan, flat: "Üzüm de yok."
6 [INGREDIENT] Selfie: he finally stands, walks to the easel and turns the phone to the almost finished portrait (him in profile under a stone arch, no writing). Long look. "Burnumu büyük yapmış." Pause. He looks at the camera. "...Doğru yapmış."
7 [NO REF] B-roll: servants carry the finished framed portrait down a long palace corridor; the painter bows deeply. VO: "Beş yüz yıl sonra Londra'da duracakmış." Beat. "Kimse bana sormadı."
8 [INGREDIENT] Outro selfie close, warm afternoon light: "Yorumlara yazın: en son ne zaman kıpırdamadan durdunuz?" Beat. "Ben üç ay." Pause. "Abone olun, kullarım."
