# Fatih Sultan Mehmet Vlog - 1 min demo, built on our shot review of "The Spanish Armada Invasion"

## What the shot review found (Armada, 10:51, 48 shots)
- 99 % AI video. Screen time: queen talking selfie 79 %, third-person (servant / observer) 9 %, B-roll without the queen 11 %, title card 1 %.
- Average shot 13.6 s, 4.3 cuts/min. Talking takes 20-30 s, held in the same framing (several generations joined by start frame). B-roll 1-15 s.
- Framing: extreme close-up 65 %, close-up 16 %. Camera fixed 58 %, moving 38 % (slow push-in / walk). Her hand or arm is visible at the frame edge in the selfies.
- Opening: 29 s extreme close-up of her face (eyes fill the frame) as the hook -> 6 s fast montage (food, servant) -> channel title card at 35 s -> walking selfie.
- Light: three-quarter key from a window or candles, warm, natural grade. Audio: voice + many small sound effects, no music bed.
- Remake plan: one generation per shot, first frame of each unit matched to the previous one (same as Flow "Kareler" with the last frame).

## Prompt formula (reverse-engineered)
SELFIE: smartphone front-camera selfie vlog, [CHARACTER LOCK], holding the phone at arm's length, hand at the frame edge, slight wide-angle distortion, [LOCATION], [LIGHT], speaks to camera deadpan with pauses: "[LINE]". Photorealistic, no text.
THIRD: photorealistic historical scene, [CHARACTER LOCK], medium shot as if filmed by a servant nearby, [ACTION], other period people around, slow push-in.
B-ROLL: cinematic photorealistic B-roll, [PLACE, YEAR], [SUBJECT], slow camera move, film grain, nobody looks at the camera, no text.

## Fatih version - "Gemileri Karadan Yürüttüğüm Gece, 1453"
16:9, Omni 1.1 Flash, 360p, 8 clips x 8 s = 48 credits. Shares like the reference: selfie 5 clips, third-person 1, B-roll 2. [ÖĞE] = FATIH reference as ingredient; [KARE n] = Flow "Kareler", start frame = last frame of clip n. Add the channel title card in the edit after clip 1 (like the reference at 35 s).
History: 22 April 1453 the Ottoman fleet (~70 ships) was hauled overland on greased wooden rollers from the Bosphorus over the Galata hills into the Golden Horn, bypassing the Byzantine chain. Mehmed II was 21. The pasha and servants are generic.

## Character reference (Higgsfield / Flow image)
Photorealistic character reference sheet, neutral grey studio background, three views (front, three-quarter, profile) of the same young man: Ottoman Sultan Mehmed II in 1453, age 21, lean young face, sharp aquiline nose, short neat dark beard and thin mustache, intense dark eyes, tall white turban wrapped around a red cap with a gold jewelled aigrette, dark green silk kaftan with sable-fur collar over a red inner robe, jewelled belt with a curved dagger. Real skin texture, no beauty filter, soft even light, identical face and clothes in all views. No text, no logo.

## Lock (start of every FATIH prompt)
Photorealistic historical vlog that looks like a real smartphone recording made in April 1453 outside Constantinople, but no phone is ever visible. FATIH exactly as in the reference image: 21-year-old Sultan Mehmed II, lean young face, sharp aquiline nose, short dark beard, intense dark eyes, tall white turban with a red cap and gold aigrette, dark green kaftan with a fur collar over red, jewelled belt with a dagger; same face and clothes in every frame. Real skin texture, no beauty filter, warm torch and lamp light, natural grade. Dry deadpan humour, confident and amused. He speaks Turkish at a natural pace, lips synced, talking or reacting until the end. Ambient sound only. No music, no subtitles, no names, no writing or letters anywhere, no modern objects.

1 [ÖĞE] COLD OPEN - Night, inside a large campaign tent lit by an oil lamp. Extreme close-up selfie: the phone is so close that only his eyes and nose fill the frame; his hand is at the edge. He whispers: "Selam olsun, kullarım. Gece yarısı." He slowly pulls the phone back to a close-up and smiles slightly: "Yarın sabah Bizans çok şaşıracak." Sound: lamp crackle, distant drums.
2 [KARE 1] Same take, no cut: he holds the phone at arm's length and tilts it to a map table with a chain drawn across a harbour (drawn lines only, no letters): "Haliç'i zincirle kapatmışlar." Beat. "Zincirden geçmeyeceğiz." Pause. "Üstünden geçeceğiz. Karadan." Sound: paper rustle, wind on the tent.
3 B-ROLL (no ingredient) - Cinematic photorealistic B-roll, night, April 1453, the wooded Galata hills above the Bosphorus: hundreds of soldiers grease long wooden logs with animal fat by torchlight while a large wooden galley with furled sails slides up the slope on the rollers, pulled by oxen and men with thick ropes. Slow camera move, film grain, nobody looks at the camera. Off-screen a calm young male voice in Turkish: "Gemi. Yağlı kütük." Beat. "Bir de çok öküz." Sound: creaking wood, oxen, men grunting, no music, no text.
4 [ÖĞE] Walking selfie up the same torch-lit hill, arm's length, his hand at the frame edge; behind him a galley slides up on greased logs and men pull ropes. Over his shoulder: "Siz buna kargo diyorsunuz." Beat. "Biz buna pazar günü diyoruz." A rope creaks loudly; he glances back, unbothered. Sound: creaking ropes, footsteps on mud.
5 [KARE 4] Same take, no cut: he turns the phone toward a sweating older pasha in a tall white turban who runs up, out of breath: "Sultanım... gemi... karada!" FATIH turns the phone back to himself, deadpan: "Biliyorum." Beat. "Ben söyledim." Sound: heavy breathing, torches.
6 [ÖĞE] THIRD-PERSON - Dawn on the hilltop above the Golden Horn, medium shot as if a young page films from a few metres away, slight shake: FATIH stands with arms crossed as a galley slides down the last slope into the calm water of the Golden Horn with a big splash; he points at it without looking at the camera: "İşte." Beat. "Suya iniyor." Sound: splash, cheering soldiers far away.
7 B-ROLL (no ingredient) - Cinematic photorealistic B-roll at dawn, 1453: Byzantine soldiers in helmets on the old stone sea walls of Constantinople stare in shock at dozens of Ottoman galleys already floating inside the Golden Horn; one drops his spear. Slow push-in, film grain, nobody looks at the camera. Sound: church bells ringing in alarm, gulls, no music, no text.
8 [ÖĞE] OUTRO - Dawn on the hilltop, close selfie, the Golden Horn full of ships behind him, wind in his kaftan: "Yorumlara yazın: siz olsanız gemiyi nasıl geçirirdiniz?" Beat. "Zincirden değil." Pause. "Abone olun, kullarım." He lowers the phone. Sound: wind, gulls.
